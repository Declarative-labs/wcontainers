# The Mesa compiler's committed plant list (AC11, the orchestrator's ruling of 2026-11-27): one plant per argument of
# every glue function of implementation.rs (the generated glue) and one per branch of the bridge's rules of a draw's
# variant (bridge.c: the position's transform, the variant's steps, nearest sampling, wraps and levels, swizzles, the
# common form's bindings, points as squares). Every plant must make the suite fail. Run from the repository's root:
#   python3 -I tools/plants/run-plants.py crates/mesa-compiler/implementation/direct/plants.txt --workers 12
# with WCONTAINERS_MESA and WCONTAINERS_SPIRV_TOOLS set (the suite builds the bridge against the pinned Mesa).
# made_variant's fragment settings (fetch_unit, fetch_multisampled, single_sampled, samples) have none: a Made is never
# a fragment program, and the passes they steer return at once on any other stage.
# Not covered by a plant here (said, not silent): the bridge's other passes (the creation steps, SPIR-V reading,
# the flattening, interpolation elsewhere, sample masks, framebuffer fetch, cube maps, gathers, storage pass-through),
# whose branches the suite's right-value tests do not yet state.
