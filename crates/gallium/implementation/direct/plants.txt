# Gallium's serving glue, its committed plant list (AC8, the orchestrator's ruling of 2026-11-27): one plant per field
# of every copy function a served pointer reaches (flat.c's wc_flat_*_from: the field dropped; flat.rs's owned: the
# value replaced), one per argument of every glue function (serve.c's trampolines, serve.rs's functions) and one per
# check of the trampolines. Every plant must make the direct crate's tests fail (the hostile caller's cases, each
# alone, also under AddressSanitizer with leak detection). Run from the repository's root:
#   python3 -I tools/plants/run-plants.py crates/gallium/implementation/direct/plants.txt --workers 10
# with WCONTAINERS_MESA, WCONTAINERS_SPIRV_TOOLS and WCONTAINERS_CLANG set. Not covered yet (said, not silent): the
# copy functions of the structures no served pointer takes (the 82 pointers not yet served), and wc_flat_pipe_rt_blend_state_from
# and wc_flat_pipe_stencil_state_from, which no trampoline calls (their structures are copied inside their holders').
